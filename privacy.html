YEVROPA OLAMI MILLIONER — Maxfiylik siyosati

Loyiha muallifi va rahbari: Jasurbek Artikov

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil 18-noyabr · English version below

Qisqacha

Ilova bilimlar viktorinasi o‘yini. Ilovada ro‘yxatdan o‘tish va reklama yo‘q. Ismingiz, natijalaringiz va sozlamalaringiz faqat qurilmangizda qoladi. O‘yinni yaxshilash uchun faqat anonim foydalanish statistikasi (Google Analytics) yig‘iladi — pastda batafsil.

Anonim statistika (Google Analytics)

Necha kishi o‘ynayotganini va qaysi mavzular mashhurligini bilish uchun Google Analytics xizmatidan foydalanamiz. U quyidagilarni yig‘adi: ochilgan sahifalar, o‘yin boshlanishi va tugashi (mavzu, daraja, ball), taxminiy mamlakat yoki shahar (IP manzil asosida, IP o‘zi saqlanmaydi), qurilma va brauzer turi, tasodifiy anonim identifikator. Ismingiz yuborilmaydi. Ma’lumotlar reklama uchun ishlatilmaydi va sotilmaydi. Batafsil: Google maxfiylik siyosati.

Qanday ma’lumotlar saqlanadi

  • Siz kiritgan o‘yinchi ismi, o‘yin natijalari, ballar, rekordlar, tanlangan til va ovoz sozlamalari.
  • Bu ma’lumotlar faqat sizning qurilmangizda (brauzer xotirasida) saqlanadi. Biz ularni ko‘ra olmaymiz.
  • Ilovani o‘chirsangiz yoki brauzer ma’lumotlarini tozalasangiz, ular o‘chadi.

Biz nimani yig‘maymiz

Aniq joylashuv (GPS), kontaktlar, kamera, mikrofon, fayllar, to‘lov ma’lumotlari — bularning birortasini so‘ramaymiz va yig‘maymiz.

Tashqi havolalar

Ilovada Telegram guruhi va do‘stlarga ulashish tugmalari bor. Ularni bossangiz, tegishli ilova yoki sayt (Telegram, WhatsApp) ochiladi. Ularning maxfiylik qoidalari o‘z egalariga tegishli.

Bolalar

Ilova umumiy auditoriya uchun mo‘ljallangan. Biz bolalardan ongli ravishda shaxsiy ma’lumot yig‘maymiz.

O‘zgartirishlar va aloqa

Siyosat o‘zgarsa, shu sahifada yangilanadi. Savollar uchun ilovadagi “Yordam va aloqa” bo‘limidagi Telegram guruhiga yozing. Dasturchining elektron pochtasi Google Play’dagi ilova sahifasida ko‘rsatilgan.

YEVROPA OLAMI MILLIONER — Privacy Policy

Project author & lead: Jasurbek Artikov

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Summary

This app is a knowledge quiz game. The app has no sign-up and no ads. Your name, results and settings stay on your device. To improve the game we collect only anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics) — see below.

Anonymous statistics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to learn how many people play and which topics are popular. It collects: pages viewed, game start and finish events (topic, level, score), approximate country or city (derived from the IP address, which is not stored), device and browser type, and a random anonymous identifier. Your player name is never sent. The data is not used for advertising and is not sold. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

What is stored

  • The player name you type, game results, scores, records, your language and sound settings.
  • This data stays only on your device (in local browser storage). We cannot see it.
  • It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

What we do not collect

Precise (GPS) location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or payment information — we do not request or collect any of them.

External links

The app contains links to our Telegram group and share buttons (Telegram, WhatsApp). Opening them takes you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. For questions, write to our Telegram group. The developer’s email address is shown on the app’s Google Play page.
