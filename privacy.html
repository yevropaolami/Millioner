YEVROPA OLAMI MILLIONER — Maxfiylik siyosati

Loyiha muallifi va rahbari: Jasurbek Artikov

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil 8-noyabr · English version below

Qisqacha

Ilova bilimlar viktorinasi o‘yini. Biz sizning shaxsiy ma’lumotlaringizni yig‘maymiz, serverga yubormaymiz va uchinchi tomonlarga bermaymiz. Ilovada ro‘yxatdan o‘tish, reklama va statistik kuzatuv (analitika) yo‘q.

Qanday ma’lumotlar saqlanadi

  • Siz kiritgan o‘yinchi ismi, o‘yin natijalari, ballar, rekordlar, tanlangan til va ovoz sozlamalari.
  • Bu ma’lumotlar faqat sizning qurilmangizda (brauzer xotirasida) saqlanadi. Biz ularni ko‘ra olmaymiz.
  • Ilovani o‘chirsangiz yoki brauzer ma’lumotlarini tozalasangiz, ular o‘chadi.

Biz nimani yig‘maymiz

Joylashuv, kontaktlar, kamera, mikrofon, fayllar, qurilma identifikatorlari, to‘lov ma’lumotlari — bularning birortasini so‘ramaymiz va yig‘maymiz.

Tashqi havolalar

Ilovada Telegram guruhi va do‘stlarga ulashish tugmalari bor. Ularni bossangiz, tegishli ilova yoki sayt (Telegram, WhatsApp) ochiladi. Ularning maxfiylik qoidalari o‘z egalariga tegishli.

Bolalar

Ilova umumiy auditoriya uchun mo‘ljallangan. Biz bolalardan ongli ravishda shaxsiy ma’lumot yig‘maymiz.

O‘zgartirishlar va aloqa

Siyosat o‘zgarsa, shu sahifada yangilanadi. Savollar uchun ilovadagi “Yordam va aloqa” bo‘limidagi Telegram guruhiga yozing. Dasturchining elektron pochtasi Google Play’dagi ilova sahifasida ko‘rsatilgan.

YEVROPA OLAMI MILLIONER — Privacy Policy

Project author & lead: Jasurbek Artikov

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Summary

This app is a knowledge quiz game. We do not collect, transmit or share your personal data. The app has no sign-up, no ads and no analytics.

What is stored

  • The player name you type, game results, scores, records, your language and sound settings.
  • This data stays only on your device (in local browser storage). We cannot see it.
  • It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

What we do not collect

Location, contacts, camera, microphone, files, device identifiers or payment information — we do not request or collect any of them.

External links

The app contains links to our Telegram group and share buttons (Telegram, WhatsApp). Opening them takes you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. For questions, write to our Telegram group. The developer’s email address is shown on the app’s Google Play page.
